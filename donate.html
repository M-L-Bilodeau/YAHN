Help Others Find the Way

You Are Here Now (YAHN) is a nonprofit organization devoted to mental health, human connection, and supporting people in real, tangible ways. Starting something meaningful like this isn’t easy—it takes time, resources, and heart. We’re just regular people with jobs, kids, and full lives—just like you.

If you'd like to help us build something beautiful, you can contribute here.

Those who donate early will be considered ground-floor supporters and will receive future benefits on any priced items or services we may offer. That said, our mission is not about profit—we will never require payment for access to this safe space. It will always be available at no cost.

Our goal is to help people find affordable care without gatekeeping or gimmicks. Building something great takes effort and funding, but we’ll never pander or compromise our values to get there.

If our mission speaks to you, please consider joining us. Thank you so much for your support.
