You Are Here Now (YAHN)

A safe space. A reminder. A movement.

YAHN is a nonprofit devoted to mental health, human connection, and real-world support— especially for those who’ve been overlooked, unheard, or left behind. We believe in meeting people where they are, and reminding them: you are here now—and that’s enough.

This isn’t just a website or a campaign. We’re building:

  • Peer support groups for trauma survivors, veterans, new mothers, and others
  • A seamless, calming digital platform that connects people without judgment
  • Local resource guides powered by AI to match people to care, food, shelter
  • An always-free space with no ads, gimmicks, or gatekeeping—just care

We’re just regular people with jobs, kids, and full plates—just like you. But we believe something extraordinary can happen when people care together.

Either way, you’re helping build something that may save a life.

Early supporters will be recognized as ground-floor champions and will receive future benefits on any items or services we may offer. But most importantly, you’ll have helped make this real.
